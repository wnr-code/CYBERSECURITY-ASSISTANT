# Market brand marks

`cloudflare.svg` and `googlegemini.svg` are the matching brand glyphs from the [Simple Icons project](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons), rendered as PNG assets for Flutter. Source glyph blob IDs at import: Cloudflare `a1cf2d6d3da27594b1862e5e9dde12e2629e0361`; Google Gemini `765adb0887e684492b7721b2d62635c11e99f7fd`. Their source files are recolored to Cloudflare orange and Google's Gemini gradient for display. The Simple Icons glyph data is provided under CC0; see `SIMPLE_ICONS_LICENSE.md`. Product names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners.

Local AI has no single third-party brand identity, so Market uses a neutral on-device icon rather than implying endorsement by another company.
